## 3. D5 RESOLVED — luminosity function (corrected; verified against
James et al. 2022b, MNRAS 516, 4862, doi:10.1093/mnras/stac2524,
§2.2.2 + Eq. 14 + Table 1; the prior v2.1 draft is RETRACTED — it
mis-derived the slope from a secondary source's phrasing)

Convention: LF in native units — observed-frame pseudo-luminosity
L [Jy ms Mpc²], L ≡ 4π D_L² F. James's E is isotropic-equivalent energy
at 1.3 GHz, effective bandwidth 1 GHz, beaming ignored; the 1.3 GHz /
1 GHz bandwidth convention versus any native-band choice is documented
as a stated misspecification axis for Phase 2b/3. Unit chain (the 4π
is ABSORBED in L by definition F = L/(4πD_L²) — do not multiply by 4π
again; a prior review erroneously "corrected" this; the chain is
dimensionally verified):
  E[erg] = 9.52×10²² × L[Jy ms Mpc²] × Δν[Hz]
  With James's Δν = 1 GHz:  E = 9.52×10³¹ × L

Form (Schechter, per James Eq. 14 — NOT a sharp-truncated power law;
they adopted it to avoid sharp-cutoff artifacts):
  dN/dL ∝ (L/L_max)^γ · exp(−L/L_max),   L ≥ L_min

Slope: γ = −1.16, the slope of the DIFFERENTIAL distribution [James
Table 1: "Slope of luminosity distribution function"; J22a: p(E) ∝ E^γ;
the integrand of their Eq. 14]. Sensitivity axis γ ∈ [−1.16, −0.77]
(J22b fitted −0.95 +0.18/−0.15). γ < −1 is bottom-weighted (L_min
matters); γ > −1 is top-weighted — the axis spans both regimes. Record:
at γ = −1.16 the detected fraction inherits E_min's uncertainty at
×1.45 per decade of E_min — one recorded sentence, not a new axis.

Bounds:
- L_max = 10^41.84 erg / 9.52×10³¹ = 7.3×10⁹ Jy ms Mpc² (log10 Emax =
  41.84, the zdm fiducial per Baptista Table 2; consistent with FRB
  20220610A, whose energy exceeds J22b's fitted Emax by 3.5–10× per
  Baptista's own note). Axis: log10 Emax ∈ [41.26 (J22b fit), 42.3].
- L_min = 10^30 erg / 9.52×10³¹ ≈ 1.05×10⁻² Jy ms Mpc² (James §2.2.2:
  E_min = 10^30 erg, "several orders of magnitude below the minimum
  burst energy of any FRB detected" — cited; replaces the earlier
  design value of 10^8).

Sanity anchors — PRINTS for human comparison, NOT gates: expected
detected fraction ≈ 2% (z=0.1), ≈ 1–1.5% (z=0.2), ≈ 0.5–0.7% (z=0.5),
≈ 0.3% (z=1), ≈ 0.1–0.2% (z=1.4); brightest z=1 burst ≈ 13 Jy ms.
STOP only on degeneracy (≈0 or ≈1), non-monotonicity of the detected
fraction in z, or disagreement with these prints beyond ~2×.

Sampling: detection is a hard fluence cut, so generate detected
catalogs EXACTLY — draw z from p_det(z) ∝ p(z)·P(L > L_t(z)), then L
from the Schechter truncated below at L_t(z). IMPLEMENTATION WARNING:
the tail uses Γ(s, x) with s = γ+1 = −0.16 < 0 at the default slope;
scipy's gammaincc/gammainccinv require a > 0 and will NaN silently.
Compute Γ(s, x) via the recurrence
  Γ(s, x) = (Γ(s+1, x) − x^s·e^{−x})/s    (s+1 = 0.84 > 0, scipy-safe),
or mpmath. Invert monotonically for truncated draws (vectorized Newton
or bisection in log x). Unit-test tail probabilities against direct
quadrature, and extend the naive-rejection cross-check to the tail
probability itself, not only the joint batch. No intrinsic rejection
loop for production.
